## Usage rules

1. Use the full-colour mark on white, near-white, midnight, or deep navy backgrounds.
2. Use the white mark over photography or complex dark surfaces when the colour mark loses clarity.
3. Use the black mark for single-colour print and accessibility-led light surfaces.
4. Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one-quarter of its visible width.
5. Do not rotate, skew, recolour individual ribbons, add shadows, or place the mark on a low-contrast background.
6. Keep the live company name separate from the mark in white-label product surfaces. This lets purchasers replace the identity without altering application layout.

## White-label hierarchy

NexusMSP uses the purchaser's uploaded wordmark first, then their uploaded app icon plus company name, and finally the NexusMSP Signal Weave as the default.

The artwork was developed as an original product identity. Formal trademark and similarity clearance is still recommended before commercial registration.
